Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Studio Chique Parlour is a hair salon located at Mallam Gbawe, opposite Radiance Fuel Station, Accra. You can reach us on 024 373 0732 about anything in this policy.

What we collect

  • Contact requests: your name, phone number and message — used only to respond to your enquiry.
  • Analytics (only with your consent): if you tap "Accept" on the consent banner, we record which pages are visited and the referring site. This is first-party and cookieless — we do not store your IP address and we do not use advertising or cross-site trackers.
  • Consent preference: your accept/reject choice is stored in your browser's local storage so we don't ask on every visit. It never leaves your device.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your personal information.
  • We never share your details with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We never send marketing messages without your permission.

How long we keep it

Contact requests are kept for up to 12 months for customer service, then deleted. Analytics records are kept for up to 12 months.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS only, and your submissions are protected in transit.

Your rights

Under the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you may ask us what personal data we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it. To exercise any of these rights, call 024 373 0732 or visit us at the address above.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new "last updated" date.
